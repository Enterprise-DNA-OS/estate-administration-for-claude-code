# Selection evidence: 19 October 2026

Estate administration was not a built family in `data/rebuilds.json`. Legal practice management already existed (`matters-for-claude-code`); this build is the estate desk inside a practice: grant dates, protection windows, claim notices, asset collection, debts, beneficiaries and the estate account.

| Registry Direct (unlisted share registry) | 1 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 3 | 16 |

Scores are judgement, not measured demand. [Estateably pricing](https://estateably.com/pricing) lists Pro at US$1,083 a month billed annually (US$12,996 a year) or US$1,204 billed monthly, for 50 new files a year with unlimited users; Growth at US$583 a month billed annually for 25 files; extra files at US$320 each on a platform plan; a US$350 onboarding fee; and notice and publication fees per estate. The page shows "$" without naming the currency; Estateably sells in the United States and Canada, so figures are written as US dollars. No NZ or AU customer invoice was verified.

The buyer fit is the strength: law firms, accounting practices and trustee companies in New Zealand and Australia run estates every week, and the protection rules are local. Estateably is built around US and Canadian probate; this build carries the NZ and NSW rules and leaves other states unconfigured rather than guessed.

[Covercy pricing](https://www.covercy.com/pricing) is per legal entity (Professional at US$406 an entity a month) and suits US real estate syndicators; NZ and AU buyer evidence was thin. Registry Direct's published plans for unlisted companies are under five figures a year.

The [inventory export guide](https://support.estateably.com/en/articles/10736208-exporting-an-inventory-estates) confirms an Excel export of each estate's assets. No export of transactions or beneficiaries was found in public help; the import covers the inventory only and says so.
